About Us

Hey everyone, I’m Myles! I’m pretty new to the off-road world, but once I got a taste of dirt trails and big tires, I was hooked. Being new means I had about a million questions—What gear should I run? How do I fix this weird rattle? Which trail won’t leave me stranded overnight?

Instead of bugging my buddies nonstop, I figured: why not create a place where rookies and veterans can swap stories, share advice, and just hang out? That’s how Trail Chatters came to life.

I may not have the most tricked-out rig (yet), but I’m here to learn, laugh, and maybe get stuck a few times along the way. If you’re into off-roading, wrenching, or just shooting the breeze about your build, you’re in the right spot.

What You Can Do Here

  • Swap trail stories with fellow off-roaders
  • Ask for mechanical tips and share your knowledge
  • Show off your rig, upgrades, and gear
  • Find guidance for trails you're exploring
  • Join a community that loves dirt, mud, and adventure

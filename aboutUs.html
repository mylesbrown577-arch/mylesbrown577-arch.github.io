My Story & Website Purpose

Hey everyone, I’m Myles Brown! I’m still pretty new to the off-road world, but once I hit my first dirt trail, felt the suspension move, and heard the engine growl, I knew this was something I wanted to stick with.

When I started out, I had tons of questions—What upgrades should I focus on first? Which trails are beginner-friendly? What tools should I keep in my Jeep? And of course… why does my rig make that weird noise sometimes?

Instead of digging through random posts or messaging friends nonstop, I wanted to create a place where I could learn, share what I find, and track my progress. That’s how this website came to life.

The purpose of Trail Chatters is simple: it’s my personal space to explore off-roading, keep notes on trails and gear, and hopefully help others who are starting their own journey. Whether it’s trail reports, upgrade ideas, or just talking about the fun (and sometimes chaotic) parts of off-roading, this site is where I document it all.

If you’re curious about off-roading, learning with me, or just enjoy seeing someone try things and sometimes get stuck, you’re in the right place.

What You Can Do Here

  • Read trail stories and experiences
  • Learn off-road basics as I learn them
  • Follow upgrades, gear lists, and progress
  • Explore trail info and guides
  • Enjoy off-road content and videos
